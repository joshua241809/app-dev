# Avengers: Endgame

A 2019 superhero film from Marvel Studios

![Avengers: Endgame poster](poster.jpg)

## A. Introduction
*Avengers: Endgame* is a 2019 American superhero film from Marvel Studios, directed by Anthony and Joe Russo. It is the conclusion of the Infinity Saga and follows the Avengers as they try to undo the damage caused by Thanos.

Avengers, assemble.


---

## B. Main Characters

### The Avengers
- *Tony Stark / Iron Man* – a genius inventor in a powered suit (played by Robert Downey Jr.)
- *Steve Rogers / Captain America* – the team's loyal and courageous leader (played by Chris Evans)
- *Thor* – the Asgardian god of thunder (played by Chris Hemsworth)
- *Natasha Romanoff / Black Widow* – a skilled spy and fighter (played by Scarlett Johansson)

### The Villain
- *Thanos* – the Mad Titan who wiped out half of all life (played by Josh Brolin)

---

## C. Plot Summary
1. Five years after Thanos erased half of all life, the surviving Avengers are still struggling with the loss.
2. The team comes up with a plan to travel through time and collect the Infinity Stones.
3. They use the stones to bring back everyone who vanished.
4. The Avengers face Thanos in a final battle, and Tony Stark sacrifices himself to save the universe.
